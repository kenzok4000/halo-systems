### Power by rail

| Rail | Loads | 5 V typical / peak | Regulator efficiency | At battery, typical / peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller 5 V | FC, GNSS, RC receiver, Remote ID | 1.80 / 2.90 W | 85% (assumed) | 2.12 / 3.41 W |
| Matek BEC 5 V | companion, thermal, RGB, smoke sensor, swarm radio | 4.17 / 9.29 W | 92% / 88% | 4.53 / 10.56 W |
| **Total** | | | | **6.64 / 13.97 W** |

The peak case adds every maximum at once: the radio sending at 1 W all the time, the companion at full load, a thermal shutter event and the smoke sensor's fan start. That does not happen in practice, but it shows the 8 W budget has no room for a continuous 1 W link. Keep the swarm radio at 100 to 250 mW over a 6 ha block.

### Endurance by the simulator's formula

`budget.py` imports `DroneBody` from `src/halo_systems/sim/body.py` and replaces mass, avionics power and battery energy in a copy. Hover power is `rotor_power_w(m g) + avionics_power_w`, with induced power `T v_h / FoM` from momentum theory over the 0.0993 m^2 disk; endurance is `60 battery_wh / hover_power_w`. Cruise is `level_power_w` at the scouting speed of 6 m/s with the simulator's 0.035 m^2 drag area.

| Case | Mass kg | Avionics W | Battery Wh | FoM | Rotor W | Hover W | Hover min | Cruise W at 6 m/s | Cruise min |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simulator defaults (body.py) | 1.200 | 8.00 | 41.00 | 0.6 | 136.5 | 144.5 | **17.0** | 126.8 | 19.4 |
| Build as modelled in CAD | 0.958 | 6.64 | 41.51 | 0.6 | 97.4 | 104.0 | **23.9** | 89.5 | 27.8 |
| Build plus 5% integration margin | 1.006 | 6.64 | 41.51 | 0.6 | 104.8 | 111.4 | **22.4** | 96.2 | 25.9 |
| Build plus margin, avionics at peak | 1.006 | 13.97 | 41.51 | 0.6 | 104.8 | 118.7 | **21.0** | 103.5 | 24.1 |
| Build plus margin, FoM 0.48 (motor and ESC losses) | 1.006 | 6.64 | 41.51 | 0.48 | 130.9 | 137.6 | **18.1** | 117.4 | 21.2 |

The simulator's figure of merit, 0.6, is a rotor figure, but `body.py` adds avionics to it and treats the sum as battery power. Real motors and ESCs lose energy as well. The last row multiplies 0.6 by an assumed 80% motor-and-ESC efficiency; hover then lands at 18.1 minutes, close to the simulator's 17.0. Measure hover current on the first build and fit the figure of merit to it (BUILD.md, step 12).
