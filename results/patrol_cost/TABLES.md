## Lightning strikes checked per patrol day

Each strike is checked as 2 measured sweeps of a 500 x 300 m block, 500 x 600 m centred on the reported strike: with the lightning network's 200 m median location error it holds about 79% of strikes (one block, about 54%). Drive-and-fly: the crew drives between strikes, at least 15 minutes a strike. Fly-between: strikes 1 km apart along a storm track, 20-minute sorties between battery swaps. Squads above 16 fly as squads of 16 in parallel, each with its own crew and ground station.
