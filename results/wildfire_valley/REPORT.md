Generated by `tools/wildfire_valley_report.py` from `runs.jsonl`: 80 flights on held-out seeds 4000-4019 (20 seeds), improved controller with the rule supervisor. Brackets are 95% bootstrap intervals over seeds. The scenario, the station and every assumption are described in `docs/WILDFIRE.md`.

## The spread forecast

The last forecast of each flight against the true spread; fewer drones sweep for longer, so their last forecast is made later, with more of the fire mapped. Sorensen index: overlap between the ground the forecast's median says will burn and the ground that does burn, counting only ground that burns after the forecast (1 is a perfect match). "Located fires" compares with the true spread of the fires the station knew were spreading (located as spreading, or mapped); "all fires" with every fire, found or not. Phone arrival error: forecast median arrival minus the true arrival, at phones the fire reaches within 3 hours (negative is early). Brier score: the forecast probability of fire within 60 minutes at each phone against what happened (0 is perfect).

## Warnings to phones

Each flight has 63 simulated phones (homes, a campground, hikers, drivers and a ranch); 5% have no signal. Alerts go to whole evacuation zones at Japan-style levels (3 prepare, 4 evacuate, 5 emergency), automatically by fixed rules once each passes the alert check, and arrive 5 to 30 s later. A phone is reached when fire comes within 50 m of it within 3 hours of take-off. Warned in time: an alert at Level 3 or above delivered before the fire arrives, as a share of the phones reached. Lead time: how long before the fire arrives the first alert lands (median over phones, then over flights). Evacuated: told Level 4 or 5.
